# Maritime Clinical Reference for RMR Review

## Airway and oxygen
If breathing is absent or insufficient gasping is observed, immediate emergency action must be documented. Oxygen delivery should match the recorded flow. Nasal cannula is appropriate at up to 5 l/min. Hudson mask is used for oxygen flow above 10 l/min.

## Breathing normal ranges
Breathing frequency is normally 12-16 breaths per minute. Oxygen saturation is normally 95-100%. Abnormal breathing should include a description such as fast, slow, shallow, deep, or normal.

## Circulation normal ranges
Capillary response should be below 2 seconds. Pulse is normally 60-80/min. Blood pressure is normally 120-140 systolic and 60-90 diastolic. Delayed capillary response should trigger documentation of venous access decision.

## Disability documentation
Level of consciousness should be coded 1-4. If consciousness is reduced or pupil reaction is abnormal, the record should describe pupil size, symmetry, and light reaction.

## Exposure and observation chart
The top-to-toe examination checks for injury or illness not recognized under A-B-C-D. Trends belong in the observation chart, including general condition, consciousness, oxygen, breathing, saturation, pulse, blood pressure, temperature, pupil reaction, and blood sugar when relevant.
